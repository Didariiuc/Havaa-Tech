# BrightRoof Energy Return Policy
## §2.1 Panels
Transit damage must be reported within 48 hours. Installed modules are excluded from change-of-mind returns. Warranty defects require serial number and installation evidence.

# EcoSpark Solar Return Policy
## §2.2 Inverters
Uninstalled inverters may be submitted for inspection within 7 days. Firmware-modified or physically damaged units require technical review and are not automatically refundable.

# SunPeak Technologies Battery Policy
## §4.1 Batteries
Battery claims require BMS logs, serial number and a qualified installer report. Incorrect charging configuration and unauthorized repair may void warranty coverage.

# Dhaka Solar Mart Returns Policy
## §3.2 Accessories
Unopened cables, mounting rails and protection accessories may be reviewed within 7 days. Cut cables and custom fabricated mounting structures are excluded.

# RenewGen BD Buyer Policy
## §1.3 Installed systems
Complete installed systems are handled through a site inspection and commissioning report. Performance disputes require irradiance, shading and load information.

# Helio Power Vendor Policy
## §2.4 Net-metering equipment
Grid-tied inverter returns require an inspection confirming no grid-code configuration changes or unauthorized installation. Net-metering approval remains subject to the relevant utility.
