# SolarWise Marketplace Buyer Protection
## §1.2 Damaged or incorrect solar equipment
Buyers should report transit damage, missing accessories or incorrect equipment promptly and provide order evidence and clear photographs. Solar panels, inverters and batteries must not be installed or modified before a transit-damage inspection. The marketplace may coordinate vendor inspection and dispute resolution.
## §1.4 Refund review
Refund requests above BDT 2,000 require marketplace administrator approval. The AI assistant may explain policy, check eligibility and create a request, but cannot approve or release a refund.
