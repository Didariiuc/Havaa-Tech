# SunGrid BD Solar Product Policy
## §3.1 Panels and inverters
SunGrid BD accepts reports of transit damage within 48 hours of delivery. Installed, physically modified or serial-number-mismatched equipment is excluded from ordinary returns unless a manufacturer defect is verified under warranty.
## §4.2 LiFePO4 batteries
Warranty claims require the battery serial number, BMS diagnostics and installer report. Capacity degradation is assessed under the manufacturer's warranty. Misuse and unauthorized modification are excluded.
